Key Findings & Recommendations
Finding 01
🏭 Kolkata Hub Bottleneck
WH-04 Kolkata East Hub averages 41.7h Order-to-Ship vs 25–30h for all other warehouses. Statistically confirmed with p < 0.0001 (Welch's t-test). Over 60% slower than the network average.
Action: Automate barcode batching & reallocate wave-picking staff
Finding 02
🚛 EcoParcel Transit Instability
EcoParcel has the widest transit spread (IQR > 35h) and the lowest on-time rate at just 17.5%. 69h average vs PrimeFreight's 27.9h — a 2.5× difference that directly drives SLA failures.
Action: Shift 30% East/Central volume to PrimeFreight
Finding 03
🎯 Systemic OTIF Failure
Overall OTIF rate is 37.2% — far below the 85% SLA target. The 3-month rolling average remains consistently under 85% confirming this is structural, not seasonal or random noise.
Action: Address WH-04 & EcoParcel simultaneously
Finding 04
📦 Stockout-Driven Cancellations
Stockout is the #1 cancellation reason alongside Customer Request and Payment Failure. Together, top 2 reasons account for ~40% of all cancellations — Pareto principle confirmed.
Action: Real-time inventory alerts for top SKUs
Finding 05
📈 Oct/Nov Festive Surges
Monthly order volume spikes sharply every October and November (41 orders peak in Nov 2025). These festive-season surges strain pick-pack capacity and worsen SLA compliance during peak periods.
Action: Pre-season staffing ramp-up plan
Finding 06
🏷️ Apparel Damage Rate
Apparel has the highest damage share at 13.8% of delivered orders, followed by Books & Media at 11.9%. Electronics shows better handling (9.5%) despite higher fragility — suggesting packaging differences.
Action: Revise packaging guidelines for Apparel
